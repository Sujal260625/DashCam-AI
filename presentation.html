From Passive Recording to Active Edge AI

Why modern vehicles need proactive collision intelligence instead of silent video files

Conventional Dashcam (Passive)

Pure Post-Crash Forensics
Silently saves video to SD cards; zero warning before impact.
No Lane Keeping or Proximity
Blind to fatigue, highway hypnosis, or sudden pedestrian crossings.
Expensive Upgrades
OEM ADAS is locked into expensive vehicles and cannot be retrofitted easily.

Our Dashcam AI Solution (Active Safety)

Real-Time Threat Warnings
Identifies danger within milliseconds with visual alerts and acoustic beeps.
Monocular Depth Estimation
Calculates metric distance with a single 2D camera lens without LiDAR.
Proximity-Adaptive Beeping
Fast, strong beeps when obstacles are near; smoothly turns silent when safe.

End-to-End System Architecture

5-stage modular perception pipeline executing synchronously on edge hardware

1. Ingestion
Dashcam MP4 / AVI video file or camera stream input.
2. Detection
YOLOv8 Nano inference identifying vehicles, cyclists, pedestrians, signs.
3. Tracking
ByteTrack Kalman filter associating persistent IDs across frames.
4. Geometry
Monocular distance math Z = (f * W) / w & Hough lane centering.
5. ADAS HUD
Threat categorization (SAFE/CAUTION/DANGER) and audio beeper.

Multi-Object Detection & Tracking

YOLOv8 Nano + ByteTrack Multi-Target Association

YOLOv8 Nano (3.2M Params)
Ultra-fast bounding box regression with minimal edge memory footprint.
ByteTrack Re-Identification
Retains track IDs across occlusions, turns, and brief camera blurs.
Ego Hood Rejection Filter
Masks out bottom windshield area to eliminate false positives on vehicle hood.

Lane Departure Warning (LDW)

Canny edge extraction, Probabilistic Hough Lines, and driving corridor centering

Dynamic Trapezoid ROI
Focuses solely on the road surface, ignoring trees, signs, and sky.
Slope-Intercept Splitting
Separates left (negative slope) and right (positive slope) lane boundaries.
Lateral Centering Offset
Alerts driver if car drifts >50 pixels off lane midpoint with rumble warning.

Monocular Distance Estimation

Pinhole camera geometry estimating depth without LiDAR or stereo rigs

Pinhole Camera Formula
Distance Z = (focal_length × Real_Width) / bbox_pixel_width
Class-Calibrated Dimensions
Car: 1.80m • Bus: 2.60m • Truck: 2.50m • Pedestrian: 0.55m • Bike: 0.80m
Relative Velocity Calculation
Computes closing speed (m/s) across a rolling 10-frame window.

Dynamic Collision Risk & Time-to-Collision (TTC)

Spatial threat categorization based on distance, closing speed, and driving trajectory

DANGER Zone (< 6.0m or TTC < 2.0s)
Immediate forward collision hazard; urgent visual badge and fast beeps.
CAUTION Zone (6.0m - 12.0m or TTC < 3.5s)
Approaching vehicle; driver alert and slow rhythmic beeps.
SAFE Zone (≥ 12.0m)
Clear forward corridor; silent operation.

Augmented Cockpit HUD & Adaptive Audio Alerts

Distraction-free visual dashboard with frequency-adaptive acoustic warnings

Top KPI Telemetry Cards
Displays Road Status, Pipeline FPS, Tracked Objects, and Lane Status.
Proximity-Adaptive Beeping
Strong rapid beeps (2200 Hz) when near; slows and turns silent when clear.
Non-Blocking Daemon Threads
Audio runs in separate threads without blocking or stuttering video replay.
